The installed Apocaplayer LateCrosshair method calls ComputeView at IL_005d, then reads eye position/forward at IL_0076/007c and IL_0093/0099 before MoveCrosshair at IL_02f4. Its installed DLL hash is unchanged. The cursor correction must therefore be present on every ComputeView request, not solely when the pose is first calculated.

## Changes

- ChaseView retains the recenter phase with its cached pose and reapplies aim synchronization on every valid TryPose request, before Apocaplayer reads its aim ray. A cached pose no longer bypasses the side effect after a later native aim write.
- DrivingAim rotates only the actual eye in the unarmed owned recenter view. Native Look state, enabled state, initialization or missing actions no longer gate eye correction.
- Eye-only native input caches in the current and standard Look/MouseSteering states are synchronized when available, including dormant controller resume. No steering/player targets or positions are written.
- Cache-writing errors warn once and leave eye correction active. A brief activation log per binding records the eye and native controller state/action count.
- Previous compass, camera ownership, zoom, cutaway, collision, smoothing and HUD implementation files are unchanged. Production edits are limited to ChaseView.cs, DrivingAim.cs and the version in Plugin.cs.
